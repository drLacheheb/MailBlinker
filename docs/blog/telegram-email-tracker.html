How to Get Real-Time Email Open Alerts in Telegram (Step-by-Step)

By drLacheheb • Updated August 2026 • 5 min read

Traditional email tracking tools notify you through noisy Chrome extension popups or cluttered web dashboards that you have to refresh constantly. If you are away from your desk, on a phone, or in a meeting, you miss the crucial window to follow up.

Telegram is the ideal notification channel for email tracking: it delivers instant push alerts to your phone, smartwatch, and desktop with zero delay.

In this tutorial, you will learn how to use MailBlinker to generate tracked emails and receive instant Telegram alerts whenever your messages are read.

Step 1: Open the MailBlinker Telegram Bot

You can either use the hosted public bot @MailBlinkerBot or launch your own private instance using the self-hosted Docker container.

Start the bot by sending the command: 

/start

Step 2: Generate a Tracked Email Link or Template

MailBlinker provides two ways to generate tracking tokens:

Method A: Quick Track Link (Command: /new)

If you want to track a quick message, send: 

/new Project Proposal | client@company.com

The bot returns a tracking pixel URL that you can embed into any email client or CRM.

Method B: Interactive Email Formatter (Command: /format)

For professional outreach, send /format. The bot guides you through a 5-step wizard:

  • Title: A reference name for your records (e.g. Consulting Pitch Q3).
  • Recipient Details: Email address and name for personalized greeting.
  • Body Text: Your pitch, proposal, or update.
  • Call-to-Action Links: Add styled action buttons (e.g., Book a Call or View Portfolio).

Step 3: Copy and Send from Gmail, Outlook, or Apple Mail

Once formatted, MailBlinker gives you clean, rendered HTML. Simply copy the rendered content and paste it directly into your Gmail or Outlook composer. The invisible 1x1 tracking pixel and anti-cache fallback tags are embedded automatically.

Step 4: Receive Rich Push Notifications in Telegram

The exact second your recipient opens your email, your Telegram bot sends an alert card:

📩 Email Opened Alert (Example)

Email: Consulting Proposal Q3

Recipient: sarah@client.com

Opened: 14 minutes after sending

Location: Paris, France (Orange ISP)

Device: Apple Mail on macOS Sonoma

Language: fr-FR (French)

Checking Statistics on Demand

To see a list of recent emails, open counts, and timestamps at any time, simply type: 

/stats
